## The flow, left to right, in 16d's layout and motion

1. **Where it comes from.** Email, Calls, PDFs icons (16d's own). Documents fly from them along
   curved paths (16d `motionPath`).
2. **The automation runs.** A panel titled "Your automation": its bars fill in steps with a
   blinking cursor (16d's typing bars), the pipe out of it flows continuously (16d `#hmPipe`).
3. **What it reports.** A panel titled "What it says": small squares travel from the automation
   into slots (16d's units). Most land and get a tick.
4. **The check, the new part.** Each square is checked against where it came from:
   - matches: it lands and ticks, quietly
   - does not match: it cracks off and falls out with the glow (16d's crack and glow), the one
     place the signal colour is used, labelled "Doesn't match"
   - cannot tell: it stops halfway, "Unsure? It stops"
5. **What the week hands back, the end of the story (founder, 2026-09-21: "what you get from our
   1000 audit ... This was the whole point").** The squares that fell out and the ones that
   stopped drop into a written sheet, "What you get back", three rows taken from the approved

   digits), "What it's costing", "Priced options to fix each one". The button under it is the
   existing "See the assessment". The sheet is the drawing's last frame, so the loop ends on the
   deliverable, not on the problem.
6. Rest 4 seconds, loop (16d `repeatDelay: 4`). Reduced motion: the settled frame.
