**Directions — Home hero, three ways.**

Argued from `design-dna.md` section 4's one idea: "the record and the reality, the claim and the
source, the pass and the miss." All three replace RULE-2026-09-16-F's 8-hours/2-hours mechanic per
RULE-2026-09-18-I. Reader, pain family and the one thing this screen says: `brief.md`.

---

## D1 — Two cards, a symbol in the gap

**Composition.** Two abstract hand-sketched records, "the claim" and "the source," on one shared
baseline. Today: nothing between them. After: a hand-drawn mark fills the gap — checkmark, signal-
blue X, or a hand-drawn question mark, one of three resolved states.

**Artifact / proof kind.** The record pair itself, drawn in the site's established hand
(`NARRATIVE.md` section 13, "a recreated record, annotated," made abstract per RULE-2026-09-18-J).
Reliability proof: the mechanism shows the system checking, not just claiming.

**Phone.** Panels stack vertically at narrow widths; the connector mark moves to sit between them
either orientation.

**Exemplar.** Builds directly on section 13's technique (round 16k, `2026-09-16k-picked-laptop.png`
and `2026-09-16k-picked-phone.png`), carried forward rather than invented. Departs from it by
staying abstract (no specific document, no client engagement) where 16k's original use was a real
recreated sheet.

Fully element-specced: `site/design/specs/hero-record-claim.md`, `hero-record-source.md`,
`hero-connector-{agree,disagree,unsure}.md`, `hero-headline.md`.

## D2 — Two cards, position instead of symbol

**Composition.** Same two panels. Agreement is shown by spatial ALIGNMENT, not an added mark:
aligned edges when the records match, physically offset or skewed when they don't, a small
unsettled wobble for can't-confirm. No third symbol vocabulary; the mismatch IS the misalignment.

**Artifact / proof kind.** The spatial relationship itself. Reliability proof, same as D1, argued
through position rather than iconography.

**Phone.** Alignment/offset has to read the same at narrow width; untested whether a vertical stack
still communicates "offset" as clearly as a horizontal pair does. Real risk, not resolved by
description alone.

**Exemplar.** Builds on `NARRATIVE.md` section 5 (motion as interaction, not decoration; the group
barely animates on load and responds richly to the pointer) — position-as-meaning is a quieter,
more interaction-native move than a popping symbol. Departs from section 13 by dropping the
annotation convention entirely.

A direction that could belong to another consultant's page is not a direction, per the chain's own
rule: D1's connector-mark shape is closer to a generic "before/after checklist" pattern; D2's
position-as-meaning has no obvious analogue in the exemplar set, which is its argument for being
tried, not its guarantee of being better.

---

## D3 — One record, an annotation that resolves

**Composition.** A single abstract record, hand-annotated at one point with a question mark today.
After: the annotation redraws into a checkmark or a signal-blue flag, in place. One object on
screen, not two.

**Artifact / proof kind.** The annotation's own transformation. Reliability proof, narrower claim
than D1/D2: it shows a single check resolving, not a comparison between two named things.

**Phone.** Simplest of the three to scale down, one object rather than a pair.

**Exemplar.** Closest to section 13's original technique, smallest departure of the three. Honest
weakness, named rather than hidden: this reads as "one record with a flag," which argues LESS
directly for "two records that should agree, and don't" than D1 or D2 do — it's plainer, not
necessarily truer to the one idea.
